**Soppeng, 6 November 2026** — Agromaterials Research Group, Politeknik ATI Makassar, together with its partner CV Gross Nusa Tech, held the first batch of technical guidance (Bimtek) on producing, installing, and maintaining *smart composite panels* made from rice husk. The activity ran from 4 to 6 November 2026 at the production line and pilot warehouse in Lajoa/Cacaleppeng, Jennae Subdistrict, Liliriaja District, Soppeng Regency. Twenty farmers from the farmer groups of Jennae Subdistrict took part.

The Bimtek is part of the ICARE (Integrated Corporation of Agricultural Resources Empowerment) Competitive Partnership (KREATIF) program of the Ministry of Agriculture for fiscal year 2026, which supports the research *Production of Rice Husk-Based Smart Composite Panels for Grain Storage Warehouses in Soppeng Regency*. Over three days the participants followed lectures on rice husk processing, environmental, social, and safety procedures, and warehouse maintenance, and then practiced operating the rice husk furnace and the hot press machine themselves.

## Why rice husk panels for grain warehouses

Grain warehouses are a critical link in the rice value chain, because they protect grain quality before it is milled or sold. Community warehouses in rice-producing areas often face large daily swings in temperature and humidity, especially in the rainy season. These swings cause condensation on walls and ceilings, raise the moisture content of the grain, and encourage mold and storage pests, which leads to lower quality, weight loss, and lower selling prices. To keep grain safe, storage practice aims for a grain moisture content of about 13 to 14 percent in a room with relative humidity below 70 percent and a temperature between 25 and 30 °C.

At the same time, rice husk, the main by-product of rice milling, is abundant in Soppeng but is mostly put to low-value uses such as simple fuel or animal feed, or is burned in the open. The smart composite panel addresses both problems at once. Rice husk is converted into porous biochar and silica-rich rice husk ash, which are formed into panels with a hydrophobic top coat that is easy to clean. The biochar works like a sponge for water vapor, absorbing it when humidity is high and releasing it slowly when humidity drops, so the panel is designed to dampen humidity swings inside the warehouse. The word smart here refers to this response of the material, not to electronics.

The first measurements at the target warehouse describe the conditions to be improved. A manual recording on 4 and 5 September 2026 found temperatures between 26.0 and 33.5 °C (average 29.9 °C), above the 30 °C limit in 18 of 40 half-hourly readings. Relative humidity stayed between 45 and 63 percent (average 50.3 percent), still below the 70 percent limit under the weather of that day. Wall moisture readings were highest near the floor on the south side, and the zinc-sheet wall on the east side was heavily corroded with traces of condensation. These figures are initial and come from a single observation period. A seven-day automatic recording with the sensor system, and monitoring in the rainy season, will confirm them before and after the panels are installed. Besides buffering humidity, the light structure of the panel is meant to reduce temperature swings of the walls.

## Mengapa panel sekam untuk gudang gabah

Gudang penyimpanan gabah merupakan mata rantai penting dalam rantai nilai padi, karena menjaga mutu gabah sebelum digiling atau dijual. Gudang rakyat di sentra padi sering menghadapi fluktuasi suhu dan kelembapan harian yang besar, terutama pada musim hujan. Fluktuasi ini memicu kondensasi pada dinding dan plafon, menaikkan kadar air gabah, serta mendorong tumbuhnya jamur dan hama gudang, sehingga mutu menurun, bobot menyusut, dan harga jual turun. Untuk menjaga gabah tetap aman, praktik penyimpanan menargetkan kadar air gabah sekitar 13 sampai 14 persen pada ruangan dengan kelembapan relatif di bawah 70 persen dan suhu 25 sampai 30 °C.

Di sisi lain, sekam padi sebagai hasil samping utama penggilingan sangat melimpah di Soppeng, tetapi sebagian besar hanya dimanfaatkan untuk keperluan bernilai rendah seperti bahan bakar sederhana atau pakan ternak, atau dibakar terbuka. Smart composite panel menjawab kedua masalah ini sekaligus. Sekam padi diubah menjadi biochar berpori dan abu sekam padi yang kaya silika, lalu dibentuk menjadi panel dengan lapisan hidrofobik yang mudah dibersihkan. Biochar bekerja seperti spons bagi uap air, menyerapnya saat kelembapan tinggi dan melepaskannya perlahan saat kelembapan turun, sehingga panel dirancang untuk meredam fluktuasi kelembapan di dalam gudang. Kata smart di sini merujuk pada respons material tersebut, bukan pada elektronik.

Pengukuran awal di gudang sasaran menggambarkan kondisi yang hendak diperbaiki. Pencatatan manual pada 4–5 September 2026 mendapati suhu antara 26,0 dan 33,5 °C (rata-rata 29,9 °C), dan melampaui batas 30 °C pada 18 dari 40 pembacaan setiap 30 menit. Kelembapan relatif berkisar 45–63 persen (rata-rata 50,3 persen), masih di bawah batas 70 persen pada cuaca hari itu. Kadar air dinding paling tinggi di dekat lantai sisi selatan, dan dinding seng sisi timur berkarat berat dengan jejak kondensasi. Angka ini masih awal dan berasal dari satu periode pengamatan. Perekaman otomatis selama tujuh hari dengan sistem sensor, serta pemantauan pada musim hujan, akan mengonfirmasinya sebelum dan sesudah panel dipasang. Selain meredam kelembapan, struktur panel yang ringan dimaksudkan untuk mengurangi fluktuasi suhu dinding.

## Getting the production line ready

Before the participants arrived, the site and the equipment were prepared. A field survey on 24 to 26 July 2026 covered the production site, the rice mill that supplies the husk, and existing community warehouses. Partner CV Gross Nusa Tech then completed the production line in Lajoa/Cacaleppeng as an in-kind contribution, including the canopy structure, roof and walls, a concrete work floor, an electrical installation with its own meter, and a production schedule board. The same partner built the rice husk pyrolysis furnace and the hot press machine in the workshop of Politeknik ATI Makassar from 3 to 29 August 2026. The two machines were tested together with the research team on 7 to 11 September, producing rice husk char, rice husk ash, and the first trial panel samples by the end of September, and were then transported to the site on 9 to 11 October 2026.
</div>

## Menyiapkan lini produksi

Sebelum peserta hadir, lokasi dan peralatan lebih dulu disiapkan. Survei lapangan pada 24–26 Juli 2026 mencakup lokasi produksi, pabrik penggilingan padi sebagai pemasok sekam, dan gudang rakyat yang sudah ada. Mitra CV Gross Nusa Tech kemudian menyelesaikan lini produksi di Lajoa/Cacaleppeng sebagai kontribusi in-kind, meliputi bangunan dan kanopi, atap dan dinding, lantai kerja beton, instalasi listrik dengan KWh meter tersendiri, serta papan jadwal produksi. Mitra yang sama membuat tungku pirolisis sekam padi dan mesin hot press di workshop Politeknik ATI Makassar pada 3–29 Agustus 2026. Kedua mesin diuji bersama tim peneliti pada 7–11 September, menghasilkan arang sekam, abu sekam, dan sampel panel uji coba pertama pada akhir September, lalu dimobilisasi ke lokasi pada 9–11 Oktober 2026.

The Head of Jennae Subdistrict, Muyassir, SE, gave a welcome address, and the Director of Politeknik ATI Makassar, Ir. Muhammad Basri, M.M., IPU, ASEAN Eng., opened the event officially. **Day 2, Thursday 5 November.** The ESF and K3 briefing continued. Muhammad Fadli Azis, S.T., M.Sc. covered the maintenance of community warehouses, and the research team presented the application of composite materials in these warehouses. In the afternoon, Ir. Cornelius Uten Patintingan, M.T. explained the operation of the rice husk furnace, including a live demonstration at the production line, with protective equipment mandatory for everyone at the production line. The participants were then divided into two practice groups.

**Day 3, Friday 6 November.** The final day was devoted to practice. After a safety briefing and the distribution of protective equipment (heat-resistant gloves, masks, and eye protection), Dr. Massriyady Massaguni, S.ST., M.T. explained and demonstrated the hot press machine. The participants then practiced operating the rice husk furnace in groups, accompanied by the research team and the partner, and in the afternoon operated the hot press machine in turns using material produced in the furnace practice. The activity closed with an evaluation and feedback from the participants through a questionnaire, and a group photo.

All participants were required to wear protective equipment during demonstrations and practice at the production line, and the furnace area was restricted to participants who were practicing. Attendance lists record gender so that participation can be reported under the ESF of the ICARE program. Participant selection aimed to be inclusive, considering small farmers, women, and young farmers, and no one under the age of 18 took part.
</div>

**Hari kedua, Kamis 5 November.** Sosialisasi ESF dan K3 dilanjutkan. Muhammad Fadli Azis, S.T., M.Sc. menyampaikan perawatan gudang rakyat, dan tim peneliti memaparkan penerapan material komposit pada gudang tersebut. Pada siang hari, Ir. Peserta kemudian dibagi menjadi dua kelompok praktik.

**Hari ketiga, Jumat 6 November.** Hari terakhir diisi dengan praktik. Setelah pengarahan K3 dan pembagian APD (sarung tangan tahan panas, masker, dan pelindung mata), Dr. Massriyady Massaguni, S.ST., M.T. menjelaskan dan mendemonstrasikan mesin cetak *hot press*. Peserta lalu berlatih mengoperasikan tungku bakar sekam padi secara berkelompok dengan didampingi tim peneliti dan mitra, dan pada siang hari mengoperasikan mesin *hot press* secara bergantian dengan bahan hasil praktik tungku. Kegiatan ditutup dengan evaluasi dan umpan balik peserta melalui kuesioner, serta foto bersama.

Seluruh peserta wajib menggunakan APD selama demonstrasi dan praktik di lini produksi, dan area tungku dibatasi hanya untuk peserta yang sedang berpraktik. Daftar hadir mencatat jenis kelamin agar partisipasi dapat dilaporkan sesuai ESF program ICARE. Pemilihan peserta diarahkan inklusif dengan mempertimbangkan petani kecil, perempuan, dan petani muda, dan tidak ada peserta di bawah usia 18 tahun.

## What comes next

The second batch of the Bimtek is scheduled for 18 to 20 November 2026 at the same location. In November the research team will also install a microclimate sensor system in the pilot warehouse to record temperature, relative humidity, and gas, which will be used to compare conditions before and after the panels are installed. The program also aims to produce operating procedures (SOP) for panel production, installation, and maintenance, and to train at least 20 farmers and warehouse operators in two batches. The activity runs until 11 December 2026.

## The program and its partners

This activity is part of the ICARE Competitive Partnership (KREATIF) program of the Ministry of Agriculture for fiscal year 2026 and is carried out by Agromaterials Research Group, Politeknik ATI Makassar. The implementing team consists of Dr. Massriyady Massaguni, S.ST., M.T. as head, Muhammad Fadli Azis, S.T., M.Sc., Irmawaty Iskandar, SP from BRMP South Sulawesi Province, Mutia Nurul Ilmi, S.T. as administrator, and the head of the farmer group in Jennae Subdistrict. The partner is CV Gross Nusa Tech, and the target community is the farmer groups of Jennae Subdistrict, Soppeng Regency. For media inquiries, write to agromaterialsrg@atim.ac.id.
