**Soppeng, 20 November 2026** — Agromaterials Research Group, Politeknik ATI Makassar, together with its partner CV Gross Nusa Tech, held the second batch of technical guidance (Bimtek) on producing, installing, and maintaining *smart composite panels* made from rice husk. The activity ran from 18 to 20 November 2026 at the production line and pilot warehouse in Lajoa/Cacaleppeng, Jennae Subdistrict, Liliriaja District, Soppeng Regency.

The Bimtek is part of the ICARE (Integrated Corporation of Agricultural Resources Empowerment) Competitive Partnership (KREATIF) program of the Ministry of Agriculture for fiscal year 2026, which supports the research *Production of Rice Husk-Based Smart Composite Panels for Grain Storage Warehouses in Soppeng Regency*. Over three days the participants followed lectures on the use of rice husk, environmental, social, and safety procedures, the maintenance of community warehouses, and the application of composite materials in these warehouses, and then practiced operating the rice husk furnace and the hot press machine in two groups.

## Why rice husk panels for grain warehouses

Grain warehouses protect grain quality before it is milled or sold, yet community warehouses often face large daily swings in temperature and humidity, especially in the rainy season. These swings cause condensation on walls and ceilings, raise the moisture content of the grain, and encourage mold and storage pests. At the same time, rice husk, the main by-product of rice milling, is abundant in Soppeng but is mostly put to low-value uses or burned in the open.

The smart composite panel addresses both problems at once. Rice husk is converted into porous biochar and silica-rich rice husk ash, which are formed into panels that are designed to absorb water vapor when humidity is high and release it slowly when humidity drops. The word smart refers to this response of the material, not to electronics. The background and the first measurements at the target warehouse are described in the [report on Bimtek Batch I]({{ '/blog/2026/11/06/bimtek-icare-soppeng/' | relative_url }}).
</div>

## Mengapa panel sekam untuk gudang gabah

Gudang penyimpanan gabah menjaga mutu gabah sebelum digiling atau dijual, tetapi gudang rakyat sering menghadapi fluktuasi suhu dan kelembapan harian yang besar, terutama pada musim hujan. Fluktuasi ini memicu kondensasi pada dinding dan plafon, menaikkan kadar air gabah, serta mendorong tumbuhnya jamur dan hama gudang. Di sisi lain, sekam padi sebagai hasil samping utama penggilingan sangat melimpah di Soppeng, tetapi sebagian besar hanya dimanfaatkan untuk keperluan bernilai rendah atau dibakar terbuka.

Smart composite panel menjawab kedua masalah ini sekaligus. Sekam padi diubah menjadi biochar berpori dan abu sekam padi yang kaya silika, lalu dibentuk menjadi panel yang dirancang menyerap uap air saat kelembapan tinggi dan melepaskannya perlahan saat kelembapan turun. Kata smart di sini merujuk pada respons material tersebut, bukan pada elektronik. Latar belakang dan pengukuran awal di gudang sasaran dapat dibaca pada [berita Bimtek Angkatan I]({{ '/blog/2026/11/06/bimtek-icare-soppeng/' | relative_url }}).

**Day 2, Thursday 19 November.** The morning covered the Environmental and Social Framework (ESF) and the operational occupational health and safety (K3) procedures, both delivered by Irmawaty Iskandar, SP, followed by a session by the research team on the application of composite materials in community warehouses. In the afternoon, Dr. Ariyanto, S.T., M.T. explained the operation of the rice husk furnace over three teaching hours, including a demonstration at the production line, with protective equipment mandatory for everyone at the production line. The participants were then divided into two practice groups and briefed on the practice sessions of the following day.

<div data-lang="en" markdown="1">
**Day 3, Friday 20 November.** The final day was devoted to practice. After a safety briefing and the distribution of protective equipment (heat-resistant gloves, masks, and eye protection), Dr. Massriyady Massaguni, S.ST., M.T. explained and demonstrated the hot press machine. The participants then practiced operating the rice husk furnace group by group, accompanied by the research team and the partner, with the furnace lit by the partner's operator before the session. After the midday break, which was extended for Friday prayers, the groups took turns operating the hot press machine using material from the furnace practice. In the afternoon, the research team, CV Gross Nusa Tech, and the ICARE facilitators of Soppeng Regency held an open discussion on cooperation with partners. The activity closed with an evaluation and feedback from the participants through a questionnaire, and a closing by the Head of the Research Team followed by a group photo.
</div>

Peserta lalu berlatih mengoperasikan tungku bakar sekam padi secara berkelompok dengan didampingi tim peneliti dan mitra, dengan tungku dinyalakan oleh operator mitra sebelum sesi. ## What comes next

With the second batch completed, both batches planned under the program have been held. The research team also plans to install a microclimate sensor system in the pilot warehouse to record temperature, relative humidity, and gas, which will be used to compare conditions before and after the panels are installed. The program also aims to produce operating procedures (SOP) for panel production, installation, and maintenance. The activity runs until 11 December 2026.

## The program and its partners

This activity is part of the ICARE Competitive Partnership (KREATIF) program of the Ministry of Agriculture for fiscal year 2026 and is carried out by Agromaterials Research Group, Politeknik ATI Makassar. The implementing team consists of Dr. Massriyady Massaguni, S.ST., M.T. as head, Muhammad Fadli Azis, S.T., M.Sc., Irmawaty Iskandar, SP from BRMP South Sulawesi Province, Mutia Nurul Ilmi, S.T. as administrator, and the head of the farmer group in Jennae Subdistrict. The partner is CV Gross Nusa Tech, and the target community is the farmer groups of Jennae Subdistrict, Soppeng Regency. For media inquiries, write to agromaterialsrg@atim.ac.id.
